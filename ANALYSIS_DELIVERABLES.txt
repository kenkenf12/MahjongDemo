    Normal: Kong declarant pays 1× fan like others
    Liability: Kong declarant pays 2× fan (or pays all players' shares)

QUESTION 4: Payment Maximum Cap
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

CURRENT SPEC ASSUMPTION:
  No cap - payments scale indefinitely with fan count
  → 10 fan hand = each loser pays 10 points (or 20 if dealer)

ALTERNATIVE 1 (Cap at Max Fan):
  Limit calculation to maximum fan value
  → Even 10 fan hand = calculated as 5 fan (if max is 5)

Example:

ALTERNATIVE 2 (Cap at Max Amount):
  Hard limit on payment amount
  → Each loser pays maximum 20 points (example)

Example:
  Max payment = 20
  Hand with 10 fan → each loser pays max 20
